# 🛒 E-Commerce Business Insights

This project uncovers actionable business insights from an e-commerce dataset using SQL. The goal was to analyze customer behavior, sales performance, and revenue distribution to support data-driven decision-making for business growth.

## 📊 Key Business Insights Extracted

### 1. Customer Engagement & Loyalty
- Identified repeat customers based on total orders.
- Highlighted customers who registered but never placed an order.

### 2. Revenue Analysis by Category & Product
- Calculated total revenue by each product category.
- Isolated high-performing categories with revenue > $5,000.
- Listed most and least expensive products within each category.

### 3. Top Customers & Customer Value
- Ranked customers by total spending.
- Segmented customers into Low, Medium, and High value tiers.
- Identified top 5 spenders for targeted marketing.
- Analyzed revenue contribution from the top 10% of customers.

### 4. Purchase Behavior & Order Patterns
- Measured average order value (AOV).
- Calculated average number of orders per customer.
- Classified orders as Small, Medium, or Large based on quantity.

### 5. Customer Acquisition & Activity Timeline
- Identified new customers acquired in 2023.
- Tracked the most recent product purchased and date per customer.
- Visualized customer spending trends over time.

### 6. Sales Trends & Seasonality
- Analyzed monthly revenue trends to detect seasonality.
- Determined the highest revenue-generating month.

### 7. Regional Revenue Distribution
- Evaluated revenue by city to identify high-performing regions.

### 8. Product Category Popularity
- Identified the most frequently ordered product category.

## 🛠️ Skills Used
- **SQL** – Complex queries, joins, aggregates, CTEs, window functions
- **Business Thinking** – Interpreting data from a strategic point of view
